How to apply

Three steps. One afternoon.

  1. 1

    Clean the crack

    Clear out loose debris, dirt and weeds. If weeds are growing in the crack, treat them with an herbicide about a week before you fill so they don't come back through.

  2. 2

    Pour it in

    Pour Tornado Crackfiller straight into the crack until it's level with the surface. The heavy-bodied formula fills the crack without running all over the pavement.

  3. 3

    Level and let it cure

    Smooth any excess flush with the included leveling tool. Let it fully cure before sealcoating, striping or driving on it.

FAQ

Common questions

1 gallon or 5 gallon?

It's the same formula in both. The bucket costs less per gallon and suits long driveways, parking pads, lots and contractor work. For a few cracks around the house, the 1-gallon jug is easier to handle.

Do you offer contractor or volume pricing?

Paving crews, sealcoaters, property managers and HOAs can ask about volume orders through the contractor form.

Do I need to mix or heat anything?

No. Tornado Crackfiller comes ready to use. Pour it straight from the jug or bucket, no melter, mixing or dilution needed.

Tornado or Gator-Nado: which do I need?

Use Tornado for individual cracks and seams. Use Gator-Nado for "alligator" cracking, where the asphalt has broken into a web of connected cracks. Gator-Nado has sand built in and is spread with a squeegee or trowel to lock those sections together.

What about weeds growing in the crack?

Pull what you can and treat the area with an herbicide about a week before applying, so nothing grows back through the repair.

Can I sealcoat over it?

Yes. Let the crack filler fully cure first, then sealcoat, stripe or open the area to traffic.

Can I use it on concrete?

Tornado and Gator-Nado are made for asphalt pavement. For damaged concrete and masonry, use Mix Master Concrete Repair Mortar, which patches, rebuilds and resurfaces concrete from a feather edge up to 1 inch (up to 4 inches when extended).
