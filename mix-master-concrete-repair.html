About this product

Patch, rebuild and resurface concrete

Mix Master has a smooth, creamy consistency that's easy to spread, shape and finish with a trowel. You can adjust it for the job: thicker and non-sag for vertical repairs, or more fluid for topping and resurfacing.

It bonds strongly, reaches up to 5,000 PSI compressive strength after 28 days and expands with heat much like concrete does, so repairs hold up. It's made to handle severe freeze-thaw cycles and works indoors or out.

Why it works

  • Two-component, polymer-modified formula for strong adhesion
  • Adjustable consistency for patching, filling, topping and resurfacing
  • Works on horizontal, vertical and overhead surfaces
  • Concrete-colored finish that blends in
  • Non-corrosive formula will not rust
  • Freeze-thaw resistant

FAQ

Common questions

How deep a repair can it handle?

From a feather edge up to 1 inch without aggregate, or up to 4 inches when properly extended.

When can I walk or drive on it?

It's open to foot traffic in about 4 hours and to rubber-tire traffic in about 12 hours.

Can I use it indoors, on walls or overhead?

Yes. Mix Master works for interior and exterior repairs on horizontal, vertical and overhead surfaces.

Which size do I need?

It comes in small, medium and large kits. If you're not sure how much your project needs, ask through the contact form.

Can I use Tornado Crackfiller on concrete instead?

No. Tornado and Gator-Nado are made for asphalt. Mix Master is the one for concrete and masonry.
